Important limits

  • Outputs are educational starting points, not legal advice.
  • Rules and deadlines can vary by country, bank, operator, ticket, route, and individual circumstances.
  • Always verify current official guidance before sending a request or claim.

Feedback and conversion signals

Open a tool, generate a local draft, download a local text file where available, or use the public feedback link in the B2B checklist. Events are counted without cookies or personal identifiers.
